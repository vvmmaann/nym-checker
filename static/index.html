⚠️ Fund your node's Cosmos account

The djibouti config-score penalty is live (since Sep 7): any nym-node holding under 1 NYM in its Cosmos (client nyx) account loses 20% of its config score - all node types. A recent drop in score or rewards is very likely this.

Keep at least 1 NYM per node (10+ recommended). It's the node's client account, not your bonding one, and the Nym API refreshes balances only ~once a day (~24h to show). Find the address on the box at <ip>:8080/api/v2/auxiliary-details · Docs ↗
